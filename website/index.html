One post, every platform.

Write one post. Postzera turns it into a ready-to-send image or video for every social platform, with a reminder and a one-tap share for each.

How it works

  1. Write onceA headline, some text, a call to action, a link and hashtags. That is all you type.

  2. See every platform’s versionEach platform gets its own caption, fitted to its limits, and its own image or video in the right shape.

  3. Send with one tapPostzera reminds you when it is time. Tap Send, pick the app, paste the caption, and mark it as posted.

Every output carries your words

Postzera never hands you plain text. Each version is an image or a video with your text already on it, laid out inside the safe area of its platform’s format. Add your own photo or video, or let Postzera make the card or slides for you. Everything is made on your phone.

Reminders that fit your day

Choose a time, and Postzera sends one notification per posting time, not one per platform. Anything you have not sent yet waits for you at the top of your queue.

Made for your phone, kept on your phone

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • Your posts, settings and files stay on your phone.
  • Works offline. Only ads and the store need a connection.
  • Available in English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Arabic, Hindi, Japanese, Korean, Chinese and Hebrew.

Pricing

Free to use. Postzera shows one small banner ad on the queue screen. One optional one-time purchase removes it for good. The price is shown in your store, in your currency.

Questions

Does Postzera post for me?

No. Postzera prepares your post and opens your phone’s share sheet. You choose the app and tap send there. It never logs in to a social platform for you and never posts on its own.

Why do I paste the caption?

Some apps ignore text that is shared from another app. So Postzera copies the caption when you tap Send, and you paste it in the app you chose.

Do I need an account?

No. There is nothing to sign up for.

Where is my data?

On your phone. See the privacy policy for the details, including what the ad service may collect.
