Privacy policy

Effective November 17, 2026

Postzera has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your posts and files stay on your phone. The only service that talks to the internet on your behalf is the ad service, and your app store handles purchases.

What stays on your phone

  • Your posts, their schedule and their send status.
  • Your platform settings and templates, and your app settings.
  • The images and videos Postzera makes, and copies of the photos and videos you choose to use.
  • A small flag that remembers whether you bought the ad removal, so ads stay off when you are offline.
  • A short diagnostic log that never contains the text of your posts. It is not sent anywhere.

We do not receive any of this. We cannot read it, and we do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, camera or microphone. Photos and videos come through your phone’s own picker, so Postzera can only use the ones you choose.

Ads

Postzera shows one banner ad on the queue screen, through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and use information from your device, such as device and advertising identifiers, your IP address, and information about how ads are shown and used. Google’s use of that information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. No ad request is made until the consent rules for your region are met. Postzera does not show ads that are treated as child-directed.

Consent in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland

If you are in one of these regions, Postzera shows Google’s consent form before any ad is requested. You can change your answer at any time from Privacy choices in Settings. If you decline, ads are served as Google’s rules allow for that choice.

Tracking permission on iPhone

On iPhone, Postzera may ask for permission to track you across other companies’ apps and websites, as the ad service requires. You can say no. You can also change your choice later in your iPhone’s privacy settings.

Purchases

The one-time purchase is handled entirely by the App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details. The store tells the app whether the purchase is owned.

Reminders

Reminders are notifications that your phone schedules by itself. No push server is involved, and nothing about your posts leaves your phone to send them.

When you share a post

When you tap Send, Postzera copies the caption to your clipboard and opens your phone’s share sheet with the image or video. The app you choose then receives them, and that app’s own privacy policy applies. Postzera never reads your clipboard.

Your choices and your data

  • Export: Settings lets you export all your posts as a CSV file.
  • Delete: Settings, Delete all data, erases your posts, platforms, settings, purchase flag and files from your phone. Removing the app does the same.
  • Rights: Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or erase on our side. For the ad service, Google is responsible for the data it collects, and its tools and policies apply.

Children

Postzera is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
