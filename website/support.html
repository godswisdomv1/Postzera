Common questions

Why do Instagram and TikTok need a paste?

Some apps accept the image or video from another app but ignore its text. When you tap Send, Postzera copies the caption to your clipboard. After you pick the app, paste the caption into its caption box. Postzera only copies when you tap Send or Copy, and it never reads your clipboard.

Why did my reminder arrive late?

To avoid asking for special permissions, Postzera uses inexact reminders on Android. In battery saving mode your phone can delay one by a few minutes. Check that notifications are allowed for Postzera in your phone’s settings. Your posts are always available in the queue, even without a reminder.

Why is a post marked Overdue?

Its scheduled time has passed and it has not been marked as posted. Overdue posts stay at the top of the queue until you send or skip them.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Settings and tap Restore purchases. Use the same store account that made the purchase, on the same kind of phone (Google Play on Android, the App Store on iPhone). The purchase does not move between the two stores.

How do I remove the ads?

Open Settings and tap Remove ads. It is a single one-time purchase.

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings and tap Delete all data. Your posts, platforms, settings and generated files are erased from your phone. Removing the app also removes everything. Before that, you can export your posts as a CSV file in Settings.

How do I move to a new phone?

Export your posts as a CSV file in Settings, move the file to the new phone, and import it there. Postzera does not back up to the cloud.

How do I change my ad privacy choice?

If your region requires it, Settings shows a Privacy choices row where you can change your answer at any time.
